# P0 Baseline (before any political system)

Engine at commit after the P0 split. To reproduce: `cd simdao_core && cargo run --release --example longrun -- <seed> 300 25`.
Machine: a 4-core cloud container, with three seeds running in parallel, so single-run speed is somewhat higher.

| seed | sim-years/s | steps/s | pop y0→y150→y300 | cultivators y300 | peak sects | sects y300 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 11.2 | 62k | ~1950 → 889 → 1413 | 355 | 162 | 71 |
| 2 | 8.8 | 49k | ~1950 → 871 → 1187 | 340 | 139 | 75 |
| 3 | 8.6 | 55k | ~1950 → 945 → 1502 | 356 | 161 | 61 |

Logged events per world per year (seed 1): human world 65, immortal realm 37, outer heaven 45, mara realm 31, sister worlds 16–28, mystic realms 2–6.
The most common event kinds are routine ones: secret-realm searches, entering and leaving seclusion, breakthroughs, beast hunts. They should become aggregated statistics, as P1–P7 plans.

Known issues carried into later phases:
- Population falls to about 45% by year 150, then recovers. The cultivator count falls steadily (900 → 350).
- The number of sects runs away (up to 162) before falling back. P1 adds the limits on founding.
- The food ledger drift grows linearly, to about -7e-4 by year 300. That is within tolerance relative to the total, but worth tracking down. Gold drift is about 3e-6 against a total of about 2.6M.
- The human world logs about 65 events per year, against a target of about 30.
